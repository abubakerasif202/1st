# 1st Class Express Marketing

Use the canonical tokens in `tokens/colors_and_type.css` and the production assets under `assets/`.

## Rules

- Preserve the industrial-editorial direction: near-black foundations, warm ivory relief sections, gold structure, and deep red conversion actions.
- Use Georgia/Cambria for display headings and Segoe UI for body copy until a licensed brand webfont is supplied.
- Prefer square or lightly clipped panels, one-pixel gold borders, and restrained shadows. Avoid generic pill cards and decorative gradients.
- Use uppercase, tracked eyebrow labels only for short category or status copy.
- Keep claims operational and qualified. Do not invent delivery guarantees, fleet capacity, client relationships, hours, or safety certifications.
- Use Lucide React icons already present in the application. Do not substitute emoji or hand-drawn SVGs.
- Use the supplied Australian linehaul photography with readable overlays and purposeful crops.
- Keep motion short and optional; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Maintain at least 44px touch targets and visible two-tone focus treatment.
