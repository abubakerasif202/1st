# Voice and tone

## Character

Professional, direct, practical, calm, and locally accountable. The brand sounds like an experienced transport coordinator: specific about the information needed, careful about promises, and easy to contact.

## Writing rules

- Use Australian English: `organisation`, `personalised`, `kilometres`.
- Use sentence case for headings and form labels. CTA labels may use title case when short.
- Keep paragraphs compact and operational. Explain the freight, route, timing, assessment, and next action.
- Use an en dash for short explanatory joins and an em dash sparingly in conversational confirmation copy.
- Format Australian phone numbers with spaces in display copy and digits only in `tel:` links.
- Do not use emoji.
- Do not publish unconfirmed hours, clients, certifications, delivery guarantees, exact capacities, or vehicle availability.
- Qualify variable services with phrases such as `subject to assessment`, `by agreement`, or `confirmed for each booking`.

## Calls to action

Primary: `Get a Free Quote`, `Request My Quote`, `Send Enquiry`.

Secondary: `Explore Our Fleet`, `Our Services`, `Discuss Your Requirements`.

Avoid vague labels such as `Submit`, `Click here`, or repeated `Learn more` links without an accessible subject.
