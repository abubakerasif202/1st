# Visual foundations

## Color

Near-black and charcoal create the dominant operational canvas. Warm ivory relief sections slow the page and support longer reading. Gold is structural—eyebrows, fine rules, borders, icons, and secondary actions. Deep accessible red is reserved for conversion actions and directional emphasis.

## Typography

Georgia/Cambria carries display headlines with tight tracking and a 1.08 line-height. Segoe UI carries body, navigation, labels, and forms. Eyebrows are uppercase, 800 weight, and tracked to 0.16–0.20em; they remain short.

## Spacing and layout

Use an 1180px maximum content width with 20px desktop and 14px mobile side gutters. Section spacing ranges from 72px on mobile to 144px on large screens. Cards use 24–40px internal spacing and share one-pixel borders rather than floating radius-heavy treatments.

## Backgrounds

Use solid dark surfaces, subtle radial light, restrained line grids, and occasional warm-ivory sections. Background effects remain behind content and never reduce text contrast.

## Borders, shadows, and radii

Gold at roughly 25% opacity is the standard dark-surface border. Light sections use muted warm-grey borders. Panels are square; photography may use a 4px radius only when presented as a framed artifact. Shadows are deep and soft, used on major imagery rather than every card.

## Motion and states

Default transitions are 180–260ms. Hover movement is limited to a 2–4px lift or a 1.025 image scale. The opening brand moment is optional and skippable. All motion collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Focus uses a light inner ring plus a dark outer ring so it remains visible on both dark and ivory surfaces. Mobile interactive targets are at least 44px.

## Imagery

Use wide Australian freight photography with real road, truck, and landscape detail. Favor a clear three-quarter truck view, restrained company-neutral trailers, and negative space for overlay copy. Apply dark overlays deliberately; do not obscure vehicle identity.

## Iconography

Production uses `lucide-react` line icons. Keep the default stroke style, use 16–20px icons in navigation and contact actions, and 38–48px icons in feature cards. Do not use emoji, icon fonts, or hand-drawn substitutes.
